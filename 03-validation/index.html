    3. Validation¶

    The public leaderboard scores one fixed set of 344,841 rows. Its result was used only to check whole models. Every decision about a component was made on 2025 data, with the rules below written before the run.

    3.1 Folds¶

    Scheme Training Validation Use
    month_loo 11 months of 2025 the held-out month; 12 folds main estimate: every 2025 row gets one out-of-fold prediction
    tail_nov_dec January to October 2025 November and December 2025 forward check: training strictly before validation
    month_jan_jul 2025 without January and July January and July 2025 early development and screening; the two months the test months correspond to

    Random K-fold splits are never used as evidence: they leak within-month conditions (weather, runway configuration, recording behaviour) between training and validation.

    Post-processors (lane head, mixture, stacker) are fitted leave-one-month-out on the members' out-of-fold predictions, with their own hyper-parameters chosen inside the training months. Their test-side models are fitted on all twelve months.

    3.2 Paired comparisons¶

    A change is always compared against a twin: the same configuration without the change, same seeds, same folds. scripts/paired_delta.py reports the RMSE difference on each row group.

    Intervals come from a day-block bootstrap: blocks are airport × UTC date, resampled within airport-month, 2,000 replicates. Errors within a day are strongly correlated (weather, runway configuration, one bad recording day at one airport). The spread between seeds understates the uncertainty by a factor of 10 to 100 and is not used as an interval.

    Every comparison is reported on:

    Row group Why
    all the objective
    ex_ro all rows without the 15 day rollovers, which hold 29 % of the final model's squared error
    January, July (ex_ro) the seasons of the two test months
    each month consistency: how many of 12 months improve
    ordinary rows with a flight-plan record, off the derived-AOBT value, label ≤ 1 h
    no flight plan the rows with the largest per-row error
    C < 1 h, C ≥ 1 h label-free split on delay against schedule (docs/01-problem-and-data.md §1.3)
    each airport no airport may lose more than 5 s without a stated reason

    3.3 Decision rules¶

    Each experiment declares its class in its configuration before it runs. The class fixes the number it is judged on; it is not changed after the result is visible.

    Class What it changes Adopted when
    ordinary features, encodings, hyper-parameters, members ex_ro interval below zero; January and July not worse (point estimate ≤ +0.5 s, interval not wholly above zero); ≥ 8 of 12 months better; forward check not worse
    tail anything that moves under 1 % of rows by more than 1,000 s all interval below zero; the ex_ro cost stated in advance and not exceeded; expected effect on the test set computed from a shrunk incidence prior, with a bounded loss if the pattern does not occur
    combination member set, stack weights nested fit; all-row and ex_ro reported for the year, January and July

    A gain concentrated in fewer than 100 rows is treated as a tail change. A new feature block must also pass a drift check: no column shifts by more than 0.25 standard deviations between 2025 and 2026 January/July without an explanation.

    3.4 How often the rule accepts noise¶

    The rule was calibrated on null comparisons (A/A tests): pairs that differ only by seed, and pairs of identical mixture fits that differ only by random initialisation.

    Null source Comparisons Accepted by "point estimate < 0 and ≥ 7 of 12 months" Accepted by "interval below zero"
    member seeds 60 37 % 7 %
    mixture refits 20 35 % 5 %

    A point-estimate rule accepts about a third of null changes. The interval rule accepts 5 to 7 %. All decisions use the interval.

    3.5 Confirmation and the winner's curse¶

    When several variants are screened and the best is kept, its screened gain is biased upward. A variant chosen on one seed is therefore re-run with fresh seeds, and only the fresh-seed result counts. Example from one selection round: a mixture setting chosen from a grid improved one member on C ≥ 1 h rows by 3.76 s [−6.95, −1.00]; with fresh seeds, across all seven members and through the stacker, it was 2.32 s worse [−0.37, +5.20] than the final model on those rows. It was not adopted.

    3.6 Hyper-parameter search¶

    Hyper-parameters were searched only where a twin comparison could confirm the result. The search (Optuna TPE) ran on January and July 2025 with one seed and the member's ordinary-row RMSE as the objective. The selected configuration was then run on month_loo with three new seeds and judged on the ten months the search never saw. Two searches passed this (RealMLP exp0268, GRU exp0271); the set-attention search did not.

    3.7 Changes to the input archives¶

    The ADS-B archive for 2025-01-01 to 2025-04-20 was rebuilt from full traces partway through the work (docs/05-reproduction.md). For a comparison between members trained on the two archive states, the validation features of January to April differ between the two sides. Such comparisons are read on May to December 2025 only, where the evaluated rows are byte-identical.

    3.8 Validation and the test set¶

    The leaderboard result was used as a check on whole candidate models. Component decisions were made on 2025 data. 2025 validation tracks the leaderboard closely for rows with C < 1 h. It does not cover one 2026 population: delayed LIRF departures in July 2026 without ADS-B ground coverage. This is why the final model is gated on C (docs/02-method.md §2.8).
