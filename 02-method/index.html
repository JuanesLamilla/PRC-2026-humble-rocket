    2. Method¶

    2.1 Overview¶

    challenge data + external archives
        │
        ▼
feature blocks (content-addressed cache)                      §2.2
        │
        ▼
members: CatBoost, RealMLP, GRU, set-attention, TabM          §2.3
  each with a recording-pattern head:  Σ p_k·C_k + (1 − Σ p_k)·ŷ
        │
        ├── routing: no-ADS-B expert on rows without a ground track   §2.4
        ▼

        ▼

        ▼

        ▼

        ▼

    Every stage after the members is a post-processor. It reads a finished run's out-of-fold and test predictions and writes a new run in the same format, so every stage is scored with the same paired comparison (docs/03-validation.md).

    2.2 Features¶

    A feature block is a function of the full movement table (2025 and 2026, departures and arrivals) that returns one row per movement. No block reads the label or the off-block time of the row it describes. Labels enter only through fold-scoped target encodings. Blocks are cached under a hash of their source code, their input and any external archive they read.

    Blocks used by every final member:

    Block Content Source
    calendar_v2, cats time of day, weekday, month; airport, runway, stand, operator, aircraft type as categoricals challenge
    fpl_deltas, fpl_updates take-off minus each flight-plan time (EOBT, AOBT, …) and their pairwise differences challenge
    regime real NM off-block, derived constant, or no NM record; which derived constant challenge
    throughput, sched_demand, runway_gaps_v2 rolling take-off and landing counts, scheduled demand, gaps between runway movements challenge
    surface_queue, discharge_v2, arrival_occupancy_v2 aircraft on the surface at pushback, take-offs and landings during the taxi window, queue position, measured taxi of neighbouring departures challenge
    arrival_taxi_in rolling taxi-in statistics of recent arrivals challenge
    stand_bounds_v2 time since the stand's previous departure and arrival: physical bounds on the off-block time challenge
    source_mismatch, identity_freq, feed_health disagreements between the airport and NM records, carrier and callsign identity, window share of rows without a flight plan challenge
    metar_visibility, winter_ops visibility, ceiling, low-visibility procedures, frozen precipitation, de-icing likelihood IEM METAR
    daylight_curfew solar elevation, local hour, minutes to and from night curfew computed
    adsb_ground from the open ADS-B track: time from first ground movement to take-off, path length, stop time, final hold, ground speed, coverage flags ADSB.lol
    adsb_ground_v2 where the track starts relative to the stand, stand-to-threshold distance, pushback signature ADSB.lol

    Added by some members: taxi_distance (taxiway-graph distance from stand to threshold; OpenStreetMap), stand_xplane (stand size class, type and heading; X-Plane Scenery Gateway), context_sets (the index used by the sequence and set models to gather nearby movements).

    Five target encodings (airport × runway, × stand, × stand × runway, × operator, × aircraft type; mean, p10, p50, count) are fitted inside each fold, on clean labels only, with shrinkage.

    The ranking set is post-operational: every take-off time is known. Queue and window features therefore use other movements' take-off times on both sides of the row. They never use another departure's off-block time or label.

    2.3 Members and the recording-pattern head¶

    Every member predicts on a square-root target and carries the same head. For each recording pattern k with a value C_k that is computable on a test row, a fold-scoped XGBoost classifier estimates p_k. The prediction is the conditional mean:

    ŷ = Σ_k p_k · C_k + (1 − Σ_k p_k) · ŷ_normal
    Pattern k Training label (from the target, training rows only) Value C_k
    schedule default |BLOCK − SCHED| ≤ 10 s MVT − SCHED, exactly the label
    day rollover |y − 86,400| < 3,600 s 86,400 s + ŷ_normal (15 positives; falls back to the base rate)
    early stamp y > 1 h with an NM record, not one of the above ŷ_normal + 1,495 s (fold-fitted mean offset)

    The normal regressor is trained on all rows with sample weight 1 − Σ p_k, where p comes from heads nested inside the training fold.

    Final members:

    Member Model Notes
    exp0250 CatBoost (depth 10, lr 0.02, leaf ≥ 100) + taxi_distance, stand_xplane; tail-prone blocks masked on no-flight-plan rows
    exp0235 CatBoost + the out-of-fold prediction of a GRU member (exp0223), built with three inner groups per fold
    exp0225 RealMLP + taxi_distance
    exp0227 / exp0268 RealMLP no masking; exp0268 uses hyper-parameters from a TPE search (§3.6)
    exp0232 / exp0271 GRU over the airport's movement sequence around the departure exp0271 tuned
    exp0233 set-attention over nearby movements
    exp0234 TabM (BatchEnsemble MLP)

    The neural members are implemented in plain PyTorch in src/prc/models/ (RealMLP after Holzmüller et al. 2024, TabM after Gorishniy et al. 2025). Each member is trained with three seeds; its test prediction comes from a fit on all twelve 2025 months.

    2.4 Routing on ADS-B coverage¶

    Roughly 40 % of departures have no ADS-B ground track: all of LTFM, most of LFPG, and LIRF in July 2026 (docs/01-problem-and-data.md §1.4). A member trained with ADS-B features predicts these rows from a missing-value pattern it has seen rarely at some airports.

    For four members a twin is trained without the 21 ADS-B ground columns (exp0259, exp0269, exp0262, exp0263). scripts/route_no_ground.py takes the twin's prediction on rows without a ground track and the base member's elsewhere. The routing flag is label-free.

    2.5 Lane head for departures without a flight plan¶

    On no-flight-plan rows every _flt column is null and the member's head loses its strongest inputs. scripts/no_nm_lane_model.py fits a two-branch estimator on these rows only:

    ŷ = p̂ · C + (1 − p̂) · ŷ_lane,        C = MVT − SCHED

    with LightGBM for p̂ and ŷ_lane, leave-one-month-out. The airports where it replaces the member are chosen inside each fold from the other months only.

    2.6 Stamp mixture¶

    scripts/mixture_v2.py models the recorded label of every departure as a mixture of six classes. The classes are training targets derived from the off-block stamp; they are never inputs.

    Class Definition Value
    default |BLOCK − SCHED| ≤ 10 s C
    far off-block more than 1 h before schedule max(C, 0) + 3600·e^a
    near C > 30 min and 10 s < |BLOCK − SCHED| ≤ 15 min C − 900·tanh(a)
    early NM record, label > 1 h (MVT − AOBT_3) + Δ_E(x)
    block ≪ flight plan NM record, AOBT_3 − BLOCK > 40 min, NM taxi in [0, 1 h] (MVT − AOBT_3) + Δ_B(x)
    normal the rest ŷ_member · e^a

    A PyTorch network outputs the class probabilities and bounded per-row value heads. The loss is the squared error of Σ p_k v_k on the raw scale plus a class log-loss. Classes that cannot occur on a row, by test-observable facts, are masked out of the softmax. Folds are nested leave-one-month-out; the test side is the mean of the thirteen fold models (--test-foldavg), averaged over two seeds. The result is blended with the member's prediction at a fold-selected weight.

    2.7 Cell stacker¶

    scripts/stack_l2.py --family cell combines the seven members with non-negative weights that sum to one, per cell of airport × flight-plan record present × C band (< 30 min, 30-60 min, 1-2 h,

    2 h). Cell weights are shrunk toward their parent (flight-plan record × band, then global); the shrinkage strength is chosen by inner leave-one-month-out. Rollover rows are excluded from every fit. The test-side combiner is fitted on all twelve months.

    Global weights of the final stack:

    Member (after lane head and mixture) Weight
    routed GRU (exp0271 / exp0262) 0.445
    routed RealMLP (exp0268 / exp0269) 0.309
    routed set-attention (exp0233 / exp0263) 0.148
    routed CatBoost (exp0250 / exp0259) 0.098
    CatBoost + GRU stack (exp0235) 0.000
    RealMLP + taxi distance (exp0225) 0.000
    TabM (exp0234) 0.000

    Members with zero global weight still carry weight in some cells.

    2.8 Gate on C¶

    scripts/gate_runs.py takes the final stack on rows with C < 1 h (87.5 % of test rows) and an earlier stack on the rest. The earlier stack has the same structure with the unrouted members (exp0250, exp0235, exp0225, exp0227, exp0232, exp0233, exp0234) and one mixture fit each.

    The reason: on 2025 validation, rows with C ≥ 1 h (rollovers excluded) score 398.0 with the earlier stack and 400.3-400.8 with the later ones. In 2026 those rows include a population with no 2025 counterpart: LIRF in July 2026, delayed, without ADS-B ground coverage. Changes that moved these rows could not be validated, so the final model leaves them on the earlier stack.
